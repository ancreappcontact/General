### 2. What Logofil is

Every day, Logofil offers one or more precise words, in English, French or Spanish, with their definition, examples, synonyms and, where relevant, a pitfall to avoid and an origin. The app includes widgets, spaced review (a daily quiz), a streak, milestones, a searchable collection and reminders.

### 3. The content

- Definitions, examples, synonyms, pitfalls and origins are **original**. They are written with the help of artificial intelligence tools, then checked before each release: the word's existence and part of speech verified against Wiktionary, an independent review by a second model, sensitive-word filters, and sample-based proofreading. We provide them with care, without guaranteeing they are entirely free of errors.
- If you spot an error, tap **“Report an error”** on the word card: the prepared email contains everything needed to fix it. Every confirmed error is corrected in a later content version.
- The suggested level and the app's scores are used to choose your words: they are not official assessments.
