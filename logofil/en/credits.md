### Word frequency

Word levels (Refined, Rich, Rare) are computed from the frequencies of **wordfreq** by Robyn Speer, whose data is licensed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq. Only frequency measurements are derived from it; no wordfreq data is reproduced in the app.

### Word verification

The existence, part of speech, gender and pronunciation of words were checked using **Wiktionary** (CC BY-SA 4.0), through the **Wiktextract** extraction by Tatu Ylonen (https://kaikki.org). No Wiktionary definition or example is reproduced: Logofil's texts are original.
