## Logofil Privacy Policy

Version 1, 26 October 2026

Logofil works **without an account, without a server, without ads and without analytics**. Your progress stays on your phone. This page explains, flow by flow, what stays on your device, the few pieces of information that leave it, and your rights.

### 1. Who is responsible?

Logofil is published by a sole trader based in the United Kingdom (the seller shown on the App Store), postal address: Lytchett House, 13 Freeland Park, Wareham Road, Poole, Dorset, BH16 6FA, United Kingdom (“we”). For any question, including about your data: [logofil.app@icloud.com](mailto:logofil.app@icloud.com).

### 2. What stays on your phone

The app stores, in its private space on your device:

- your settings: word and app language, pace, reminder time, themes, level, voice, light or dark theme;
- your onboarding answers (motivations, calibration);
- your progress: words seen, known, kept and mastered, review dates, streak, milestones;
- the word schedule for the next 30 days.

This information is processed on your device, under your control. **We have no access to it**: it is never sent to us or to any provider. Words, definitions and examples ship with the app: showing or searching for a word goes through no server.

- **Widget.** The app writes the word schedule to a space shared with its widget, on your phone. The widget shows the word of the day and its definition on your screen: anyone who sees your screen can read them.
- **Pronunciation.** The 🔊 button uses your iPhone's speech synthesis, which runs on the device.
- **Reminders.** These are local notifications, scheduled by your phone itself, with no notification server.

### 3. Backups and exports

- **iCloud Backup.** If you turned it on, your iPhone backup includes Logofil's data, like that of your other apps. Apple keeps it in your iCloud account under its own rules; we have no access to it.
- **Export** (Settings › My data › Export my progress). It creates a readable JSON file with your progress and settings. You choose where to save or send it; the service you choose may keep a copy under its own rules. **Importing progress** replaces the data on the phone with the file's data.

### 4. What leaves your phone

**4.1 Your purchases.** Payments are processed by **Apple** (App Store), with your Apple Account and under its own privacy policy. We receive neither your name, nor your email address, nor your payment details.

To verify and restore your purchases without a Logofil account, we use **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), acting on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

- a random identifier created by the app, not linked to your name;
- purchase information sent by Apple: product, dates, price, currency, storefront country, transaction identifiers;
- technical information related to each connection: IP address, app and system version, language.

This data is **pseudonymised**, not anonymous: it relates to specific purchases. No data about your progress or your words is sent.

- **Why**: to give you access to what you bought, restore it, and handle the trial, renewals and refunds.
- **Legal basis**: performance of the contract between us.
- **Retention**: as long as your subscription or lifetime access must be verifiable and restorable, then for the duration of our accounting and legal obligations.
- **Transfer**: RevenueCat hosts this data in the United States. This transfer is covered by its data processing agreement, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

**4.2 Crash reports.** Logofil includes no crash-reporting tool. If you agreed, in your iPhone settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymised technical reports under its own rules. You can change this choice at any time.

**4.3 When you write to us** (“Contact us” or “Report an error”). The email is prepared in your mail app: you review it and you send it. For a report, it contains the word's identifier, its language and the app and content version numbers. We receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying and correcting the content, or performance of the contract if your request is about a purchase. Retention: as long as needed to handle your request, then at most 3 years after our last exchange, unless the law requires longer.

**4.4 What Logofil doesn't do.** No account. No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifier. No usage analytics. No online artificial intelligence. Your data is never sold.

### 5. Erasing your data

**Settings › My data › Erase everything** deletes Logofil's data from this device: progress, settings, schedule, widget and reminders. This action:

- doesn't cancel your subscription: cancel it in your Apple Account settings;
- doesn't delete exports you made or older backups of your phone;
- doesn't delete purchase data held by RevenueCat (see section 6).

Deleting the app also erases its private space.

### 6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the GDPR (European Union), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

- **Data on your phone**: you control it directly in the app (view, change, export, erase).
- **Purchase data at RevenueCat**: write to us at [logofil.app@icloud.com](mailto:logofil.app@icloud.com) with your order number, shown on Apple's receipt (“Order ID”). It lets us find your record without any other information. We reply within one month and only ask for ID in case of serious doubt. Deleting this data doesn't cancel your subscription and may prevent restoring it.
- **Complaints**: you can contact a data protection authority. In the UK, the ICO: https://ico.org.uk; in the European Union, the authority of your country of residence, for example the CNIL in France (https://www.cnil.fr) or the AEPD in Spain (https://www.aepd.es).

### 7. Children

Logofil collects no personal data about its users, whatever their age. Purchases by minors follow Apple's rules (Family Sharing, Ask to Buy).

### 8. Changes

This policy may change, for example when a feature changes. The version in force is the one shown in the app, with its number and date. Any significant change will be announced in advance, in the release notes or in the app.
