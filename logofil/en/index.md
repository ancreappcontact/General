# Logofil

**The right word, every day.** One precise word a day on your lock screen, and a 2-minute review so you never forget it. In English, French and Spanish.

## Support

A question, a mistake in a word, a request about your data: write to us at [logofil.app@icloud.com](mailto:logofil.app@icloud.com). We usually reply within 48 hours.

- **Report a definition error**: in the app, tap “Report an error” on the word card: the email contains everything we need.
- **Restore a purchase**: Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases, with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
- **Manage or cancel your subscription**: Settings › Manage subscription in the app, or iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
- **Move to a new phone**: Settings › My data › Export my progress, then Import on the new phone.
- **Delete your data**: everything stays on your phone. Settings › My data › Erase everything, or delete the app.
- **Add the widget**: touch and hold the lock screen › Customize › add the Logofil widget.
