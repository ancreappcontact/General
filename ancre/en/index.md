# Ancre Support

A question, a problem, a request about your data: write to us at [ancreapp.contact@gmail.com](mailto:ancreapp.contact@gmail.com). We usually reply within 48 hours.

- **Restore a purchase**: in the app, Me › Settings › Subscription › Restore purchases, with the Apple or Google account used for the purchase.
- **Manage or cancel your subscription**: iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions; Android, Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
- **Delete your data**: everything stays on your phone. In the app, Me › Settings › Erase my data from this phone, or delete the app.

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services: 112 in Europe, 999 in the UK, 911 in North America.
