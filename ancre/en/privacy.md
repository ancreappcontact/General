## Ancre Privacy Policy

Version 2 of 9 October 2026

Ancre is designed so that **what you write stays on your phone**: the app doesn't send your journal, answers, reasons or history to any server. This page explains what stays on your phone (iPhone or Android), the little information that leaves it, and your rights.

### 1. Who is responsible?

Ancre is published by Valentin Groff, sole trader, Lytchett House, 13 Freeland Park, Wareham Road, Poole, Dorset, BH16 6FA, United Kingdom ("we"). For any question, including about your data: [ancreapp.contact@gmail.com](mailto:ancreapp.contact@gmail.com).

### 2. What stays on your phone

What you enter is stored in the app's private storage, on your device:

- your first name (optional), your year of birth and your answers to the questionnaire;
- your plan, what you chose to quit and your commitment signature;
- your check-ins, mood, notes and journal;
- your urges, their triggers and your relapses;
- your reasons: texts, photos and voice memos;
- your progress, reflections and monthly check-ins.

This information is processed on your device, under your control. **We have no access to it**: it is never sent to us or to any provider. The personal statistics shown in the app are calculated on your phone.

**Protection.** The app's files are protected by your phone's encryption, which relies on its passcode. Ancre's lock (face, fingerprint or passcode), if you turn it on, protects access to the app's screens: it doesn't encrypt the data with a separate key, and your phone's passcode can also open it.

**"Talk to Ancre" coach** (iPhones that support Apple Intelligence). It is Apple's artificial intelligence running on your iPhone (Apple Foundation Models). To tailor its answers, it uses, on the device, your number of days, your main triggers, what you chose to quit and your written reasons. Your messages aren't sent anywhere. The conversation stays in memory while the screen is open, then it's erased; no human reads or monitors it. Elsewhere, a guided coach, without artificial intelligence, also works offline.

**Reminders.** They are local notifications scheduled by your phone itself, with no notification server. Their texts are neutral.

**Widget.** It shows your number of days and neutral labels on your screen: anyone who sees your screen can read them.

**Help.** The numbers offered depend on the country set on your phone; this setting is read on the device.

### 3. Backups and exports

- **iCloud Backup (iPhone).** If you turned it on, your iPhone's backup includes Ancre's data, like that of your other apps. Apple keeps it in your iCloud account under its own rules; it is end-to-end encrypted only if you turned on "Advanced Data Protection". We have no access to it.
- **Google backup (Android).** Ancre excludes its database and files (journal, history, reasons, photos, memos) from Google backup; only a few technical preferences may be included. To change phones, use the export.
- **Export** (Settings › My data › Export my data). It creates a JSON file, **readable in plain text**, with your texts and progress. It **doesn't include** the files of your photos or voice memos. You choose where to save or send it; the app or service you choose (messaging, cloud storage…) may keep a copy under its own rules. **Import a backup** replaces the data on the phone with the data in the file.

### 4. What leaves your phone

**4.1 Your purchases.** Payments are processed by **Apple** (App Store) or **Google** (Google Play), with your account there and under their own privacy policies. We don't receive your name, email address or bank details.

To check and restore your purchases without an Ancre account, we use **RevenueCat** (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), which acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

- a random identifier created by the app, not linked to your name;
- the purchase information sent by Apple or Google: product, dates, price, currency, store country, transaction identifiers and receipts;
- technical information tied to each connection: IP address, app and system version, language.

This data is **pseudonymized**, not anonymous: it relates to specific purchases. Ancre connects to RevenueCat as soon as it opens, so that a purchase can be restored; no other data is sent at that moment.

- **Why**: to give you access to what you bought, restore it, and handle the trial, renewals and refunds.
- **Legal basis**: performance of the contract between us.
- **How long**: as long as your subscription or one-time purchase must be checked and restored, then for the duration of our accounting and legal obligations.
- **Transfer**: RevenueCat hosts this data in the United States. This transfer is covered by its data processing agreement, which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

**4.2 Crash reports.** Ancre doesn't include any tool that sends crash reports. If you agreed, in your phone's settings, to share analytics with app developers (iPhone) or usage and diagnostics data (Android), Apple or Google may send us technical crash reports, without the content you entered, under their own rules. You can change this choice at any time in those settings.

**4.3 When you write to us.** If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write or attach. Don't include intimate information we don't need. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, or performance of the contract if your request is about a purchase. How long: the time needed to handle your request, then at most 3 years after our last exchange, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

**4.4 Protection against adult websites.**

- **iPhone**: the Safari blocker uses a list of sites built into the app, applied by Safari on your iPhone. Ancre never sees your browsing.
- **Android**: the app guides you to turn on Android's "Private DNS" yourself with the free filter from **CleanBrowsing**, a service independent of Ancre. This setting applies to your whole phone: the names of the sites and services your phone contacts are then sent to CleanBrowsing instead of your carrier. Ancre receives nothing about your browsing. The setting stays on even if you uninstall Ancre: to stop it, choose "Automatic" in Android's Private DNS. CleanBrowsing's policy: https://cleanbrowsing.org/privacy

**4.5 Sites and numbers opened from the app.** When you open a website from Ancre (a help resource, for example) or call a number, that website or service receives your request as with any browsing or call.

**4.6 What Ancre doesn't do.** No account. No ads, no ad tracking, no advertising identifier. No usage analytics tool. Your data is never sold.

### 5. Erasing your data

**Settings › My data › Erase my data from this phone** deletes Ancre's data from this device: database, private files (photos, memos), cache, widget and reminders. This action:

- doesn't cancel your subscription: cancel it in your Apple account settings or in Google Play;
- doesn't delete the exports you made or older backups of your phone;
- doesn't delete the purchase data held by RevenueCat (see section 6).

For the most complete deletion, then delete the app: the system destroys its private storage.

### 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR (European Union) and the UK GDPR (United Kingdom), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

- **Data on your phone**: you control it directly in the app (view, edit, export, erase).
- **Purchase data at RevenueCat**: write to us at [ancreapp.contact@gmail.com](mailto:ancreapp.contact@gmail.com) with your order number, shown on the receipt from Apple ("Order ID") or Google (number starting with "GPA."). It lets us find your record without any other information. We reply within one month and only ask for an ID document if there is serious doubt. Deleting this data doesn't cancel your subscription and may prevent restoring it.
- **Complaints**: you can contact a data protection authority. In the United Kingdom, the ICO: https://ico.org.uk ; in the European Union, the one in your country of residence, for example the CNIL in France (https://www.cnil.fr) or the AEPD in Spain (https://www.aepd.es).

### 7. Adults only

Ancre is for people aged **18 and over**. Age is declared in the app, without identity checks. If you think a minor is using Ancre, write to us.

### 8. Changes

This policy may change, for example when a feature changes. The version in force is the one shown in the app, with its number and date. Any significant change will be announced in advance, in the update notes or in the app.
