### 2. What Ancre is

Ancre is a wellness app for adults who want to reduce or stop a compulsive use of pornography. It includes a 90-day program (one lesson a day), then "Course 365" (one lesson a week until week 52 and a check-in every month), tools (Storm mode, check-ins, journal, reasons, personal statistics, widget, reminders), a coach, protection guides and help resources.
